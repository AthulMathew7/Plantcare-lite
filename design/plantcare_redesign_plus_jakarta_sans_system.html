Common Problems

Powdery Mildew

White, powdery fungal growth on leaves and stems. Weakens plants by blocking sunlight.

Downy Mildew

Yellow leaf spots with fuzzy gray/purple undersides. Thrives in cool, wet conditions.

Rust

Rust-colored pustules on leaves/stems, causing premature leaf drop.

Clubroot

Swollen, distorted roots; stunts plant growth.
